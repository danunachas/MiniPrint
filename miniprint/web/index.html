Calibrate support tips

    
Every test piece is a small bar floating above the plate on its supports: its flat underside is the 90° test and one end is a 45° slope, both supported with one combination of tip diameter and tip penetration. Rows are tip diameters, columns are penetrations; each piece shows its values on top in hundredths of a millimetre (t48 = 0.48 mm tip, P20 = 0.20 mm penetration). Everything else uses your current support settings — set them up as you usually print first.

    

    

    

    

    
After printing, look at each piece: did all tips hold while printing (no sagging or loose strands under the flat underside)? Do the supports snap off cleanly? How big are the marks on the slope and on the underside? Take the tip diameter and penetration of the best piece, enter them in Supports (Tip penetration is under More settings) and save them in a profile.
